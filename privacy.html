Privacy Policy — Telesales Agent CRM

Last updated: November 2026

Telesales Agent CRM is a call-report app for telesales agents. It helps agents keep a call list, mark call status (Connected / Not Connected), mark validity (Valid Yes / No), add remarks, and generate daily reports.

Information the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app receives your Google account name and email address to identify your agent account. The app uses Google Sheets and Google Drive only for your own telesales data: it reads and writes the call-list spreadsheet shared with you, and it reads, creates, and updates your own report and status-log spreadsheets in your Google Drive. The app does not access any other files in your Drive.

How your information is used

Your information is used only to run the app for you: showing your call list, saving the statuses and remarks you enter, computing your daily counts, and generating your daily reports. Your data stays in your own Google Sheets and Google Drive; the app has no separate server that stores your data.

What the app does not do

The app shows no advertisements, uses no analytics, and does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party. It never reads your Gmail, contacts, photos, or any file you have not shared with it.

Data security

Access to your data is protected by your own Google account security and by Google's OAuth permissions, which you can review or revoke at any time in your Google Account settings. If you revoke access, the app can no longer read or write your sheets.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The updated version will always be posted on this page.
